# Escape (Unity 6)
Race against rising hazards across different maps, trigger switches to unlock exit routes, and reach the safe zone before the chamber is submerged.

---

## Goal
Navigate through obstacle courses, find and touch all required switches to open blocked paths, and reach the exit zone before the rising hazard catches up to you.

---

## Rules
* **Outrun the Rising Hazard:** Lava, acid, or water continuously rises throughout each stage. Falling into the hazard results in an instant loss.
* **Activate All Switches:** Locked doors, gates, and bridges require you to locate and touch buttons along the route to progress.
* **Precision Platforming:** Narrow wall ledges, floating platforms, and suspended bridges require careful jumps—falling off the main path ends the run.
* **Reach the Exit Zone:** A round is only complete once you cross the final stretch and enter the safe exit area.
